Terms of Service

1. Roast-to-Order Integrity

Every single-estate micro-lot is roasted strictly to order during our nocturnal roasting cycles in Bengaluru. Because our products are fresh artisanal foodstuffs, cancelled orders cannot be revoked once the roast stasis chamber cycle has initiated.

2. Connoisseur Guarantee & Replacements

If any harvest lot arrives with seal compromise or does not meet your sensory expectations, our Master Sommelier will dispatch a complimentary replacement micro-lot within 24 hours.

3. Indian Postal Dispatch & GST Compliance

All pricing is in Indian Rupees (INR) inclusive of applicable GST. Tax invoices bearing your registered corporate GSTIN are generated upon request.
