Our Ethos & Manifesto

About Wakey Owl:
Vision & Mission

We are on a relentless pursuit to elevate Indian specialty coffee to the highest echelons of global luxury, uniting ancestral terroir wisdom with futuristic cryogenic roasting science.

✦

Our Grand Vision

To establish Indian high-elevation, shade-grown Arabica as the definitive benchmark for luxury coffee globally—demanding the same reverence, pedigree, and collectible status as Grand Cru Burgundy wines and rare single-malt whiskies.

For centuries, the finest Indian micro-lots were exported quietly to luxury salons in Milan, Tokyo, and Paris. Wakey Owl ensures that India's elite patrons experience the undisputed finest harvest first.

⚖

Our Sacred Mission

To engineer an uncompromising luxury coffee experience by guaranteeing zero bitterness through precision cryogenic roasting, while paying 300% over market prices directly to our partner planters in the Western Ghats.

Every bean is harvested by hand at peak brix sugar levels, roasted only between 00:00 and 05:00 UTC under stable nocturnal atmospheric pressure, and sealed in nitrogen stasis.

The 4 Pillars of Excellence

Our Operational Philosophy

1. Planter Sovereignty

We operate through multi-year direct agreements with 5th generation estate families in Chikmagalur and BR Hills, funding advanced anaerobic fermentation tanks and raised drying beds.

2. Nocturnal Science

At midnight, atmospheric barometric pressure stabilizes. Our Bangalore roastery operates in nocturnal shifts to ensure 0.00% thermal drift during caramelization.

3. 100% Shade Canopy

We exclusively source from estates maintaining biodiverse bird-friendly shade canopies in the UNESCO Western Ghats, ensuring zero clear-cut deforestation.

4. Connoisseur Guarantee

If any roast does not awaken your sensory palate with complete harmony and zero harsh astringency, our master sommelier will personally replace your allocation.

The Nocturnal Roastery Lab

Headquartered in Bengaluru, India

Our central laboratory combines Giesen fluid-bed roasting technology, cryogenic liquid nitrogen stasis tanks, and precision spectrometry cupping facilities.
