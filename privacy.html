Data Sovereignty & Encryption

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India) and GDPR.

1. Connoisseur Data Collection

We collect essential logistics information (recipient name, dispatch address, PIN code, and contact coordinates) solely for executing precision roast batch allocations and priority air express delivery across India.

2. Payment & Cryptographic Security

Payment transactions (UPI, luxury credit cards, netbanking) are processed via 256-bit TLS encrypted bank gateways with zero raw card data stored on our servers.

3. AI Sommelier Query Privacy

Queries submitted to our AI Sommelier & Encyclopedia are processed anonymously in-session to calibrate flavor recommendations and brewing ratios without profiling.

4. Concierge Contact

For data inquiries or deletion requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@wakeyowl.luxury.
