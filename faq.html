Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our high-altitude Western Ghats sourcing, nocturnal cryogenic roasting, and royal brewing rituals.

Between 00:00 and 05:00 UTC, ambient atmospheric barometric pressure in Bengaluru stabilizes to optimal density. This elimination of daytime thermal draft allows our fluid-bed roasters to execute exact caramelization curves with 0.00% thermal drift, locking in delicate fruit esters and eliminating bitter chlorogenic astringency.
In 1670 AD, Sufi saint Baba Budan planted seven sacred Yemeni beans on the Chandragiri Hills of Chikmagalur. We partner exclusively with 5th generation estate planters who continue this 400-year-old tradition under native silver oak and wild cardamom canopies at elevations exceeding 1,200m to 1,750m.
For authentic brass Davarah extraction, select our Fine-Medium South Indian Filter Grind. Use an 85% Arabica PB and 15% slow-roasted chicory blend (our Mysore Imperial Blend). Tamp lightly and pour 92°C water for a 15-minute slow gravitational drip yielding a thick, velvet first decoction.
Immediately post-roast, whole beans are rapidly quenched using sub-zero nitrogen stasis and hermetically sealed in matte obsidian canisters with positive nitrogen pressure. This completely displaces oxygen, halting oxidation and preserving peak aromatic bouquet for 90 days.
All orders above ₹1,500 enjoy Complimentary Royal Express Air Courier. Orders placed before midnight are roasted fresh in that night's cycle and dispatched via Bluedart Priority Air next morning. Major metros (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai) receive deliveries within 24 to 36 hours.
