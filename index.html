The 400-Year Legacy

Seven Sacred Beans &
The Western Ghats

In 1670 AD, the revered Sufi saint Baba Budan smuggled seven raw coffee beans from the port of Mocha in Yemen and planted them upon the Chandragiri Hills of Chikmagalur.

Today, Wakey Owl honors this sacred origin by partnering with 5th-generation heritage planters across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Andhra Pradesh, elevating Indian coffee to the global haute couture luxury stage.
